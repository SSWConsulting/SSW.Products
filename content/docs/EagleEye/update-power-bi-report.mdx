SSW releases new versions of the SSW EagleEye - Power BI template app. Your installed app does not update itself. This guide shows you how to update it and keep your database connection, refresh schedule and portal embed link.

## Prerequisites

* You installed the app as [Step 4 - Integrate Power BI report](./step-4-integrate-power-bi-report) describes.
* You are an Admin of the Power BI workspace that holds the app.
* A new version is available:
  * If you installed from the Microsoft Marketplace, Power BI shows an update banner in the workspace and a notification.
  * If you installed from a link that SSW sent you, SSW tells you about the new version.

## Update the app

1. Turn off Large storage format. Power BI can't update a template app whose semantic model uses it.
   1. In Power BI, open your EagleEye workspace.
   2. Next to the **EagleEye** semantic model, select **More options (...)** → **Settings**.
   3. Expand **Large semantic model storage format**. If the toggle shows **On**, set it to **Off** and click **Apply**.
      ![Large semantic model storage format toggle set to On](/EagleEye/Guides/Power-BI-Large-Storage-Format.png)
2. Click **Get it** on the update banner or in the notification. You can also open the [SSW EagleEye - Power BI](https://marketplace.microsoft.com/en-us/product/power-bi/ssw1700450141896.ssweagleeye-power-bi) page on the Microsoft Marketplace and click **Get it now**.
3. Click **Install**. The **Update app** dialog opens.
4. Select **Update only workspace content without updating the app**. This option keeps your workspace name and app name.
5. In **Select the workspace to overwrite**, select your EagleEye workspace. Power BI preselects a workspace for you, and it can be a different one.
   ![Update app dialog with Update only workspace content selected and the EagleEye workspace chosen](/EagleEye/Guides/Power-BI-Update-Workspace-Only.png)
6. Click **Install**. Power BI shows **Your workspace was updated!**
7. Wait for the refresh to finish. Power BI starts it by itself, and a large database can take 30 minutes or more. Until then, the report shows sample data.
8. In the workspace, click **Update app**, then click **Update app** and **Update**. Your users now see the new version.

The update keeps these settings, so you don't set them again:

* Your report ID, so the embed link in your EagleEye Portal keeps working.
* Your database parameters and credentials.
* Your refresh schedule.

## Check the update

* In the semantic model **Settings** → **Refresh history**, the top refresh shows **Completed**.
* In the workspace list, the semantic model shows a date under **Next refresh**.
* The Power BI report in your EagleEye Portal shows your data.

## Troubleshooting

**"This app cannot be updated, choose a different app or install another copy of the app."**

The semantic model uses Large storage format. Do step 1, then start again from step 2.

![This app cannot be updated error in the Update app dialog](/EagleEye/Guides/Power-BI-Update-Blocked.png)

**The workspace or the app has a new name, such as "SSW EagleEye Template" and a date**

You selected **Update the workspace and the app**. To fix it:

1. Rename the workspace in **Workspace settings**.
2. Rename the app in **Update app** → **Setup**.
3. Check that the report still shows in your EagleEye Portal. If it doesn't, copy the embed link again with steps 8 to 11 of [Step 4 - Integrate Power BI report](./step-4-integrate-power-bi-report).

**The refresh fails**

1. Open **Refresh history** and read the error.
2. Check the database credentials, as [Manage Credentials for Database](./manage-database-credentials) describes.
3. If the refresh still fails, contact SSW and include the error text.

**No date under Next refresh**

Power BI turns off the refresh schedule after 4 failed refreshes in a row. In the semantic model **Settings** → **Refresh**, turn the schedule on again.
