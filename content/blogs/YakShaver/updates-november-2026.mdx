Across seven releases published from 26 October to 23 November, YakShaver made it easier to get each shave to the right person and the right place. This update covers product changes included in releases published between **23 October and 26 November 2026, Australia/Brisbane**.

## YakShaver - 4 New Features

### Sign in to a YakShaver Widget

Anyone recording through a YakShaver Widget on another site used to be anonymous, so their shaves were credited to the widget. Team members can now sign in to a widget with their YakShaver account, and the shaves they record there are credited to them. The sign-in is remembered for that site and browser.

The widget shows who you are recording as at the top. If you are not signed in, it warns you and offers **Sign in**. You can sign out from the widget, or record as a guest and go back to your account later.

### Move a shave to another project

A shave that landed in the wrong project could only be moved from its Teams card, and desktop and YakShaver Anywhere shaves have no Teams card. The **More actions** menu on My Shaves and the admin shaves page now has **Move to...**, next to **Retry**. Pick the target project and YakShaver moves the shave. It is available to the shave's owner and to admins.

### Teams cards show the title and project before you choose

When you send a recording through Teams, the project selection card now shows the work item title YakShaver proposes, the project it thinks the recording belongs to, and the people the recording mentions. The card only offers projects that are set up for YakShaver Anywhere, so you can't pick one that has nowhere to file the work item.

### Recordings stay private until they are published

YakShaver Anywhere recordings are now stored privately and read through short-lived signed links, instead of sitting at a link anyone could open. A published copy on your Video Host is public, and people signed in to the same YakShaver tenant can view the private recording. If a project uses the default OneDrive / SharePoint Video Host, the project form now tells you that videos there are published at a public link.

## A couple of useful fixes

Developers embedding YakShaver Anywhere can start a recording from their own app and attach structured context, such as the page URL or record ID, so the work item uses those values. Work items also mention the project's Primary Contact and the people named in the recording, and transcription is given the project's people so their names are spelled correctly.

A project's Shaves tab now searches, sorts and pages across all of its shaves, not just the page on screen. If YakShaver can't tell whether a Zendesk ticket was created, you can check and file it again from the portal.
