Here's a look at November's YakShaver updates, from widget sign-in to moving shaves between projects, including the releases up to 23 November.

## 📦 YakShaver Updates

### 🙋 Shaves credited to the right person

- **Widget sign-in:** Team members can sign in to a YakShaver Widget with their YakShaver account, so the shaves they record there are credited to them. The sign-in is remembered for that site and browser.
- **Know who you are:** The widget shows who you are recording as, warns you when you are not signed in and offers **Sign in**.
- **Sign out or go guest:** Sign out from the widget, or record as a guest and switch back to your account later.

### 📦 Shaves in the right project

- **Move to...:** The **More actions** menu on My Shaves and the admin shaves page has **Move to...**, next to **Retry**. Pick a project and YakShaver moves the shave. Available to the shave's owner and to admins.
- **Teams cards show more:** The project selection card shows the work item title YakShaver proposes, the project it guessed and the people the recording mentions.
- **Only usable projects:** The Teams card only offers projects set up for YakShaver Anywhere.

### 🔒 Private recordings

- **Private until published:** YakShaver Anywhere recordings are stored privately and read through short-lived signed links. People signed in to the same YakShaver tenant can view them.
- **Public Video Host notice:** A project on the default OneDrive / SharePoint Video Host now shows that videos there are published at a public link.

### 🩹 Fewer snags

- **Search every shave:** A project's Shaves tab searches, sorts and pages across all of its shaves, not just the page on screen.
- **Names spelled right:** Transcription is given the project's people, so their names come out correctly.
- **Better mentions:** Work items mention the project's Primary Contact and the people named in the recording.
- **Zendesk re-filing:** If YakShaver can't tell whether a Zendesk ticket was created, you can check and file it again from the portal.

**Developer note:** Apps that embed YakShaver Anywhere can start a recording and attach structured context, such as the page URL or record ID, so the work item uses those values.
