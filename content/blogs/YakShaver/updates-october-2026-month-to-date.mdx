Finding the right recording guide should be straightforward. This October, YakShaver's website received a fix for an old guide link, clearer document structure and improvements to its sitemap.

This **month-to-date update covers 1–22 October 2026, Australia/Brisbane**. It focuses on website and documentation changes verified on the public site on 22 October.

## An old recording-guide link works again

If you saved a link to `/docs/setting-up-other-recording-tools`, it now redirects to [Using YakShaver with Third-Party Recording Tools](https://yakshaver.ai/docs/third-party-tools-setup). You can keep using that bookmark, or update it to the current address.

The [redirect fix](https://github.com/SSWConsulting/SSW.Products/pull/826) was merged on 12 October. The destination remains the existing setup guide; this change repairs access to it.

## Link directly to a section

On the recording guide, use the link icon beside a section heading to get its direct address. For example, you can share the [troubleshooting section](https://yakshaver.ai/docs/third-party-tools-setup#troubleshooting) when helping someone check their recording setup.

October's [heading changes](https://github.com/SSWConsulting/SSW.Products/pull/834) also give documentation pages a main page heading and separate the sidebar navigation labels from the document's heading structure. The [shared heading-link pattern](https://github.com/SSWConsulting/SSW.Products/pull/835) uses the icon beside the heading as the link.

## More guides in the sitemap

The website's [sitemap update](https://github.com/SSWConsulting/SSW.Products/pull/827), merged on 22 October Brisbane time, fixes a content-pagination problem that left documentation pages out of the sitemap. The live [sitemap](https://yakshaver.ai/sitemap.xml) now includes the third-party recording guide, mobile recording guide and individual recording-tool guides. These are existing guides being included in the sitemap.

Looking for help with your setup? Start with the [YakShaver documentation](https://yakshaver.ai/docs) or open the [third-party recording guide](https://yakshaver.ai/docs/third-party-tools-setup).
