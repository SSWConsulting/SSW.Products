A customer emails your team about a late delivery. A week later they email again, and this time they're angry. Nobody escalates it, because it's one email among thousands. You find out when they cancel.

The warning was there, in writing. It usually is. Your team's email, GitHub issues and pull requests already answer the questions you ask every week: are customers getting happier or angrier? Are we following our own process? Which projects are stuck? But nobody has time to read it all, so it just sits there.

## What is EagleEye?

EagleEye reads your company's email and GitHub activity every night, tags what matters with AI, and turns it into reports. It runs in your own Azure, so your email stays inside your company.

## Tell it what to look for

A tag is something you want to find. Create an "Angry customer" tag, describe it in plain English, and AI finds those emails however people word them. For simple things, use a rule, like "Approved by" in the email. The same tags work across email and GitHub.

## See the trends

* 📊 **Email** - how many emails got each tag, week by week
* 📈 **GitHub** - the same tags on issues and pull requests, so you can see which ones are waiting for a decision or blocked, repo by repo

## Or just ask

Connect EagleEye to Claude or GitHub Copilot over MCP and ask:

> Are angry customer emails going up, and how many new issues are waiting for a decision?

It's read-only and off by default, and needs a one-time Microsoft Entra setup. EagleEye can also call a webhook when a new email, issue or pull request gets a tag, so you can act on it straight away.

## Your data stays yours

* EagleEye installs from the Azure Marketplace into your own Azure subscription
* The AI service is yours too, and you pick the model
* The email body isn't kept: only the subject, people, date and tags
* SSW has no access to your data
