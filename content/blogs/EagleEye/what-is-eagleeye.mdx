Every company has its own language. "Checked by", "Test passed", "As per our conversation" - the phrases your team uses in email say a lot about how work actually gets done. EagleEye finds them for you.

## How it works

EagleEye scans your company's email every night and tags it. You decide what to look for:

* 📃 **Non-AI tags** match simple rules, like a phrase in the subject
* 🤖 **AI tags** understand context, so they catch the idea even when the wording changes
* 🤖 **AI with Mentions tags** also pick out who is mentioned in the email

Need to look further back? Run a historical scan from the Portal and EagleEye goes through past email too.

## See it in Power BI

The results land in Power BI reports built for you: emails sent, emails received and emails with mentions, each broken down by tag. No spreadsheets, no manual searching.

## Your data stays yours

EagleEye runs on Azure resources in your own tenant. You control who has access, how it's encrypted and where it lives. It works with Microsoft Exchange and GitHub, with more integrations on the way.
