Every company has its own language. "Checked by", "Test passed", "As per our conversation" - the phrases your team uses in email, issues and pull requests say a lot about how work actually gets done. EagleEye finds them for you.

## What EagleEye reads

EagleEye scans your company's work every night:

* 📧 **Email** from Microsoft Exchange
* 🐙 **GitHub** issues, pull requests and their comments

Need to look further back? Run a historical scan from the Portal and EagleEye goes through past activity too.

## You decide what to look for

Tags tell EagleEye what matters to you, and the same tags work across email and GitHub:

* 📃 **Non-AI tags** match simple rules, like a phrase in the subject
* 🤖 **AI tags** understand context, so they catch the idea even when the wording changes
* 🤖 **AI with Mentions tags** also pick out who is mentioned

## See it in reports

* 📊 **Email** - Power BI reports of emails sent, received and with mentions, broken down by tag
* 📈 **GitHub** - an Overview for issues and for pull requests: what was raised and closed, how many are waiting, and how long they take

No spreadsheets, no manual searching.

## Put it to work

* ⚡ **Webhooks** - give a tag a webhook, and EagleEye calls it when a new email, issue, pull request or comment gets that tag
* 🤖 **AI assistants** - turn on MCP access in Settings, and ask your AI assistant about your EagleEye data. It's read-only and off by default

## Your data stays yours

EagleEye runs on Azure resources in your own tenant. You control who has access, how it's encrypted and where it lives.
