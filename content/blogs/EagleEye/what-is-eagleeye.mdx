A customer emails your team about a late delivery. A week later they email again, and this time they're angry. Nobody escalates it. It's one email among thousands, in someone else's inbox. You find out when they cancel.

The warning was there, in writing. It usually is. But nobody can read every email the team gets, and search only finds what you already know to look for.

## What is EagleEye?

EagleEye reads your team's email and GitHub every night, and uses AI to find the things you care about, like an angry customer, however they word it.

## Catch the angry customer in time

Describe what you're looking for in plain English: "a customer who is frustrated, threatens to escalate, or chases a missed reply". From then on:

* 🔔 Each new one can go to a Microsoft Teams channel the next morning, so someone picks it up
* 📊 You see whether it's getting better or worse, week by week

## Same idea, on GitHub

Decisions stall in issue comments too. EagleEye finds the issues waiting on someone to decide, and who that is.

![GitHub issue mentions in the EagleEye demo, filtered to the Needs decision tag](/EagleEye/Blogs/what-is-eagleeye-github-mentions.png)

Figure: AI found the decision and who it's waiting on: "Waiting on Nadia and Rajiv for the call on whether to spend a sprint on the proper fix or patch it for now."

## What else can it find?

Whatever you can describe in a sentence. For example:

* ⏳ A customer question nobody has answered yet
* 💸 A customer disputing an invoice
* 🚨 Emails and GitHub issues about a security incident
* 🙌 A customer thanking someone on your team

## Your email stays in your company

Email is sensitive. EagleEye runs in your company's own Microsoft Azure, not ours, and the AI service is yours too. The email text isn't kept, only the subject, people, date and tags. SSW can't see any of it.

## What you need

* Microsoft 365 for email, GitHub for issues and pull requests, or both
* An Azure subscription. EagleEye installs from the Azure Marketplace, after a one-time app registration in Microsoft Entra
